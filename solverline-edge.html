How this works (manual)
  1. Tap UFC / MLB below. That builds the answer key: fair win % for every side, from de-vigged sportsbook consensus. One tap each.
  2. Go to the Kalshi tab and tap a series button. Every open contract shows as Yes/No price boxes. Tap a box → instant verdict (EV per contract, fees in) matched automatically to the fair %.
  3. Pick'em lines (Underdog/PrizePicks): tap the leg's "Add to slip" then check the Slip tab for true parlay EV. "To win" singles → Check tab, two numbers.
  4. Manual entry exists under each event only for stuff no feed shows (F1 props etc). It's the fallback, not the main path.
