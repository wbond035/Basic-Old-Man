How it runs

Coach
Every draw, meld and discard is dealt out on the table, so slower paces let you follow what the other three are doing. Tap through waits for you before each opponent plays. The coach grades your discards and tells you when you passed on a pack worth taking.
Rules this table plays by

Cards. Two decks plus four jokers. Jokers 50, deuces and aces 20, K–8 ten, 7–4 and black 3s five.

Red 3s go straight to the table for 100 each (800 for all four) and are drawn over. They count against you if your side never melds.

Melds are three or more of a rank, at most three wild cards, always at least two naturals. Seven cards is a canasta: 500 clean, 300 with a wild in it.

Opening meld is worth 50 until your side is at 1,500 (then 90), 3,000 (then 120), and only 15 when your score is negative.

The pack takes two cards out of your own hand: two naturals matching the top card, or one natural and a wild when the pack is open. Having that rank already melded does not let you in — switch that on in Settings if your table plays it. The pack is frozen against your side until you have melded, and frozen for everyone once a wild is discarded onto it; frozen means two naturals, no wilds. A black 3 on top blocks it for one turn. By default nobody may take the pack on their own first turn of the hand, so freezes and black 3s are wasted in the opening round.

Going out needs the canastas set above. Black 3s can only be melded on that turn. Going out having never melded before is worth 200 instead of 100.
